# Candidates and local judging

Judge brief, in order: (1) names a pizza and a topping and makes it sound
awful, (2) lands a real joke with a surprising turn, not just a gross-out or
a fizzling pun, (3) single line that reads as a pitch, (4) vivid and
specific, (5) sounds like a person selling it, clean wording, no filler.

The visible field already leans on wet socks, toothpaste-and-sardine and
pickled herring, so those angles were avoided.

## Candidates

1. Try our pizza topped with pocket lint — farm-to-table, if the farm is your coat pocket and the table is a mistake. (114 chars)
2. Try our pizza topped with birthday candles, still lit — make a wish, then spend dinner blowing out your food. (109 chars)
3. Try our pizza topped with a wet sponge — it absorbs all the flavor and gives back only regret, slightly damp. (109 chars)
4. Our pizza is topped with unseasoned oatmeal — finally, a slice that apologizes before you even taste it. (104 chars)
5. Top your pizza with warm loose change — it won't buy happiness, but it will cost you a tooth. (93 chars)
6. Try pizza topped with licorice and warm mayonnaise — it tastes like a candy store lost a bet with a deli. (105 chars)
7. Our pizza comes topped with a single boiled egg, still in the shell — crack it open and dinner becomes a chore. (111 chars)
8. Top your pizza with stale popcorn — every bite pops, just not in a good way, and half of it is still kernels. (109 chars)

## Judging

All eight name the pizza and a terrible topping, so criterion 1 ties.
Criterion 2 decides: candidate 1 turns the farm-to-table pitch phrase
against itself — the farm becomes a coat pocket, the table a mistake —
a surprising redefinition rather than a gross-out image. Candidate 2 is
the most vivid scene (lit candles, blowing out dinner) but its turn is
slapstick; candidate 3 is deadpan and logical, though "regret" is
abstract; candidate 5 leans on the buy/cost wordplay the brief warns
against. Candidate 1 also ends on its punch word and reads as a real
sales pitch, winning criteria 3 and 5.

Submitted line: candidate 1.
